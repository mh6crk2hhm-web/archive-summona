What counts as free to sample here

In the United States, sound recordings published before are in the public domain, and the line moves forward a year each January. Early records are only drawn when dated or earlier, which leaves a year between recording and release. Other countries have different rules. Each recording's label shows why it is here; read its record page before you release anything built on it. This is a guide, not legal advice.
